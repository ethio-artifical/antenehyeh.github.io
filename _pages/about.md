I am Anteneh Yehalem, a Lecturer and Researcher in the Department of Data Science at Bahir Dar University, Ethiopia. I also work as a Data Science and Analytics Bootcamp Trainer at Baacumen (remote) and have industry experience as a Machine Learning Engineer.

My work focuses on building practical and low-cost AI systems with impact. My research interests include computer vision, natural language processing, multi-modal data processing, modular deep learning, fine-tuning large language models, and multi-agent AI systems.

I hold an M.Sc. in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science from Bahir Dar Institute of Technology, where my thesis focused on end-to-end continuous Ethiopian Sign Language recognition, and a B.Sc. in Computer Science from Debre Tabor University.

## Current Focus
- Computer Vision and NLP research for real-world Ethiopian and African contexts
- End-to-end learning approaches for sign language and speech applications
- Applied machine learning projects in healthcare, transportation safety, and agriculture

## Publications and Research
I have authored and co-authored multiple peer-reviewed papers, including publications at ICT4DA 2025 (IEEE), with work spanning sign language recognition, Amharic speech recognition, machine translation, and EEG-based seizure detection.

I am also actively involved in ongoing research projects, including road crash severity prediction, coffee leaf disease detection, brain tumor classification, and emotion-aware recommendation systems.

## Teaching and Service
- Teaching deep learning, machine learning, and data visualization
- Mentoring students and coordinating research collaborations
- Serving as a reviewer for CHI 2026 (poster session) and ACVSS 2025
